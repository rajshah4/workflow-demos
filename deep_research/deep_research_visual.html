💡 The Key Pattern

Unlike traditional single-agent workflows where the agent coordinates turn-by-turn, the workflow script holds the plan. Intermediate results stay in script variables instead of Claude's context, allowing the final answer to be clean and focused. This enables complex research tasks that would be impractical in a single agent.
