💡 Why Parallel Reviewers?

Traditional code review is sequential - one person checks everything. With parallel AI reviewers, each expert examines their domain simultaneously. Security finds what performance misses, style catches what security overlooks. The synthesis step then prioritizes by severity, giving you actionable feedback in minutes instead of hours.
